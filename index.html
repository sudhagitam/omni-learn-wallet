Topic: Linear Equations & Desmos Shortcuts

QUESTION 1 (MATH ALGEBRA)

For the function f(x) = 4x - 9, what is the value of f(5)?

EXPLANATION

f(5) = 4(5) - 9 = 20 - 9 = 11.
Desmos Tip: Type f(x)=4x-9 then f(5) on line 2!
