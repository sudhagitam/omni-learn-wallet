MATH PRACTICE Q1 (ALGEBRA)

If 3x + 7 = 22, what is the value of 6x - 4?

Desmos Solution: 3x + 7 = 22 → 3x = 15 → x = 5. Substituting into 6(5) - 4 = 26.
MATH PRACTICE Q2 (SYSTEM OF EQUATIONS)

A system consists of 2x + y = 10 and x - y = 2. What is the value of x?

Desmos Solution: Add both equations: (2x + y) + (x - y) = 10 + 2 → 3x = 12 → x = 4.
MATH PRACTICE Q3 (GEOMETRY & TRIANGLES)

A right triangle has legs of length 6 and 8. What is the perimeter of the triangle?

Solution: Hypotenuse c = √(6² + 8²) = √(36 + 64) = √100 = 10. Perimeter = 6 + 8 + 10 = 24.
READING PRACTICE Q4 (VOCABULARY IN CONTEXT)

"The scientist remained pragmatic despite emotional pressure." What is the closest meaning of pragmatic?

Explanation: Pragmatic means dealing with matters sensibly and realistically based on practical considerations.
WRITING PRACTICE Q5 (SENTENCE TRANSITIONS)

Solar panels generate clean electricity during sunny days; ______, energy storage batteries are required for nighttime power.

Explanation: "However" correctly establishes a contrast between daytime solar generation and nighttime battery storage needs.
