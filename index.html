If 3x + 7 = 22, what is the value of 6x - 4?

Step-by-Step Solution:
1. 3x + 7 = 22 → 3x = 15 → x = 5.
2. Substitute x = 5 into 6x - 4 → 6(5) - 4 = 30 - 4 = 26.
